Vote by mail

You can apply online to vote by mail and track the status of your application.

  1. Apply online and wait for approval — it can take up to four days to process. Once approved, you cannot choose another voting option.
  2. You'll receive a voting kit with a ballot at the mailing address you provide.
  3. Return it in the prepaid envelope, or drop it off at your local election office.
  4. Elections Ontario must receive your completed kit by 6 PM (Eastern) on election day, Sep 3, to be counted.
